The `members` field is an array of objects. Each member object needs `name`, `role`, and `skills` fields.

---

The `role` field must be exactly one of: "lead", "senior", or "junior". Watch for typos and capitalization.

---

The `skills` array must contain at least one skill. Skills are simple strings.

---

The `deadline` must be in YYYY-MM-DD format. Example: "2025-03-15"
