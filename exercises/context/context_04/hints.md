Read through the conversation looking for points where the user or agent commits to something. Phrases like "let's go with", "I've decided", "we'll use" signal decisions.

---

Facts are different from decisions. Facts are discoveries: "the API returns XML not JSON", "the budget is $500/month", "the deadline is Friday". These constrain future actions.

---

Current state should answer: "If someone took over this conversation right now, what would they need to know to continue effectively?"

---

Open items are questions asked but not yet answered, or decisions mentioned but not yet made. They're the "TODO" list for the conversation.

---

For metadata, estimate token counts: original conversation tokens and your summary tokens. Even rough estimates (original ~2000, summary ~400) demonstrate you understand the compression achieved.
